Electrical Engineering @ VJTI · 2nd year

I like machines.

The ones with wires, motors, gears, sensors, and a tendency to stop working for reasons you definitely should have noticed.

I’ve always been drawn to hardware and anything that turns an idea into something physical. But what really gets me curious is autonomy — watching a machine go from “I’m controlling it” to “it figured it out.”

That’s what has pulled me into robotics and robot learning.

Before this, I spent a while in a very different rabbit hole: BlazeScript, a C++ compiler project. It taught me a lot about systems, C++, and the surprisingly fun process of figuring out things I initially knew nothing about.

Now I’m doing it again, just with robots.
